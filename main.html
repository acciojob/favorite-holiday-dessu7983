Manali


Manali is one of the most beautiful hill stations in India, located in Himachal Pradesh. It is famous for its snowy mountains, adventure sports, green valleys, and peaceful atmosphere.

Top 5 Attractions

  1. Solang Valley - Famous for adventure activities.
  2. Rohtang Pass - Known for stunning snow-covered views.
  3. Hadimba Temple - A beautiful ancient temple.
  4. Old Manali - Popular for cafes and scenic beauty.
  5. Beas River - Perfect place for river rafting.

Must-Try Foods

  • Siddu - Traditional Himachali dish.
  • Trout Fish - A famous local delicacy.
  • Thukpa - Delicious Tibetan noodle soup.
  • Momos - Popular steamed dumplings.
  • Babru - Himachali-style stuffed bread.
