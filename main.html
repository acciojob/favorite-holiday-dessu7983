Manali


Manali is one of the most beautiful hill stations in India, located in Himachal Pradesh. It is famous for its snowy mountains, adventure sports, green valleys, and peaceful atmosphere. Thousands of tourists visit Manali every year to enjoy nature and relax.

Top 5 Attractions

  1. Rohtang Pass - Famous for snow activities and amazing views.
  2. Solang Valley - Popular for paragliding and skiing.
  3. Hadimba Temple - A beautiful ancient temple surrounded by forests.
  4. Old Manali - Known for its peaceful cafes and scenic beauty.
  5. Beas River - Perfect place for rafting and photography.

Must-Try Foods

  • Siddu - A traditional Himachali dish.
  • Thukpa - A delicious hot noodle soup.
  • Momos - Popular steamed dumplings.
  • Trout Fish - Famous local fish dish.
  • Babru - A tasty Himachali snack.
